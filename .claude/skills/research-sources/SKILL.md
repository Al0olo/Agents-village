# Research sources

1. Open the source yourself (WebFetch or WebSearch). Do not rely on a summary, a search
   snippet or memory for a number.
2. Record it in `docs/research/sources.md` with its reading level: *Read in full*, *Read at
   source*, *Abstract level*, or *Cited, not re-checked*.
3. In prose, claim no more than the reading level supports. Abstract level means abstract
   claims only. Say "reported" for numbers you did not reproduce.
4. If you find a claim in the docs that a source does not support, fix the doc and note the
   correction in `docs/handoff/LOG.md`.
5. Check what the paper does not do, as well as what it does; novelty claims need the
   closest prior work named.
6. Never fabricate a citation, identifier or quote. If you cannot open it, mark it
   unverified.
7. Link papers; do not copy PDFs into the repo.
