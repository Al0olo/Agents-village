# Research sources

Every paper and report the design documents cite, with how closely it was read. Papers are linked, not copied: most are under licences that do not allow redistribution, and the canonical copy is the better one to cite.

**Reading levels.** *Read in full* means the whole paper apart from references. *Read at source* means the paper or its page was opened and the figures used in the docs were checked. *Abstract level* means the abstract was read and only abstract-level claims are made. *Cited, not re-checked* and *secondary summary* mean the claim comes from another source and should be verified before it is relied on.
