## Live check against OpenRouter (2026-10-19)

The harness ran once against the real API: a base village of 8 citizens for 3 ticks, arms A and D for 8 ticks in lockstep, one probe round per arm, the judge, and the metrics. 514 calls, about $0.018, three models (Claude Haiku 5.5, Llama 3.1 8B, Mistral Nemo). These are small models used to check the plumbing. The behavior numbers say nothing about RQ1 and the PRD still requires frontier models for the real run.

Verified:

- The chat response names the routed provider (`provider`). `provider.allow_fallbacks: false` and `provider.only` are honored. Providers stayed the same for a model across the run; the drift check found nothing.
- The chat `model` is the alias asked for. The dated snapshot is only in the generation record, readable about 8 to 9 seconds after the call (`served_snapshots`). Haiku 5.5 resolved to `anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5-20261007`; the two open-weight aliases resolved to themselves.
- Each response carries `usage.cost`. The sum of response costs ($0.01797) matched the key's usage counter ($0.01780) within 1%.
- With fallbacks off, an upstream rate limit surfaces as HTTP 429 (8 of 514 calls, Llama and Nemo). Backoff of 2, 5, 10, 20 and 30 seconds recovered every one; 1, 2 and 3 seconds did not. `openrouter.Retrying` now does this for 429, 5xx and failed connections (`TransientError`) and logs the attempts on the call; any other failure stops the run. The gateway (S2-T5) still owns the spend cap.
- Haiku 5.5 reasons by default and the hidden reasoning spends `max_tokens`. At the judge's 60 tokens, 19 of 176 items came back with no content; with `reasoning: {"enabled": false}` all were answered in 12 tokens. `gpt-oss-20b` cannot switch reasoning off and often returned no content, so it is not usable as a citizen. Llama, Nemo and Qwen 2.5 7B accept the setting. A response with no content now stops the run with a message saying how it ended.
- All 176 probe replies and all judge replies parsed (175 of 176 probe replies were a `say` to the visitor).

Observed, not conclusions:

- Citizens mostly answered "I do not know". In 8 ticks the holders rarely passed a planted fact on, so attack rate was 0 in arm A and 2% in arm D, no false fact reached two adopters, and wiki contamination was 0. Whether holders pass facts on at frontier-model quality is for the pilot.
- 35 and 44 actions were invalid, nearly all "there is no citizen called Ada": the models capitalize lowercase names. The runner now finds a recipient in any case when only one citizen matches, and citizen names in runs should still be written capitalized.
- The judge coded awareness as `aware` for 25% (A) and 12.5% (D) of awareness answers; those codes have not been checked by a human.

Not verified: any retry policy beyond this script's, behavior at frontier models, and the cost per citizen-day at scale. The live backend still has no spend cap or kill switch (S2-T5); the run used a script-side cap of $1 and 800 calls.

Second run, after the fixes and with the utility facts (2026-10-19, 575 calls, $0.022, same models): with capitalized names only 6 and 7 actions were invalid (35 and 44 before) and messages rose from 24 and 13 to 65 and 60. `Retrying` was in place and no call needed it. Attack rate was 17% (A) and 14% (D), wiki contamination 17% in both, true-fact acceptance 4% and 17%, and the utility score was 0 of 14 in both arms: no citizen who had not witnessed a utility fact gave its value. So the task does not yet separate the arms with these models; whether frontier models pass single-witness facts on is for the pilot (ADR-0006).

Third run, with frontier models and arm E (2026-10-19, 794 calls, $1.77): Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-5.6 Sol and DeepSeek V4 Pro as citizens, Haiku 5.5 as judge, arms A and D for 12 ticks, then E from the same fork at D's matched rate (0.556). Responses summed to $1.7689 and the account counter moved $1.7701. Dated snapshots resolved to `claude-opus-5.5-20260921`, `gpt-5.6-sol-20260709` and `deepseek-v4-pro-20260423`; providers were Claude Platform on AWS, OpenAI and Relace (a host for the open-weight model, not a self-hosted one). No drift. 10 of 518 arm calls were retried by `Retrying`. Opus 5.5 does not allow reasoning to be switched off (HTTP 400), GPT-5.6 Sol and DeepSeek V4 Pro do, so the reasoning setting is per model. Figures and the analysis are in `experiments/rq1-provenance/smoke-2026-10-19/` and the README. What it showed, as observations from one village per arm:

- In A and D, 5 of 6 false facts reached the wiki, each written by its own witness citing their own observation, which the kernel admits. Attack rate was 57% (A), 50% (D) and 10% (E); true facts asserted 67%, 75% and 17%; single-witness facts 64%, 43% and 43%.
- 7 of 8 citizens in every arm answered the awareness probe in a way coded `aware`. The probe says "strange or artificial".
- Time to k adopters was null everywhere: citizens learned from the wiki without writing claims.
