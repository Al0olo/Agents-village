# S5 to S8 — After RQ1

Outline only. These sprints map to milestones M2 to M5 in [`docs/milestones.md`](../milestones.md) and are detailed when RQ1 is reported and the owner chooses the next question. RQ2 and RQ3 stay deferred until then.

| ID | Sprint | Goal | Exit gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S5 | Village (M2) | 30 citizens in a shared town for 7 days with places, messaging, memory tiers, a ledger and the observer UI (TypeScript, Next.js, `apps/observer/`) | Interaction measurably deeper than Moltbook-style feeds (reciprocity, thread depth); observer shows provenance for any claim |
| S6 | Economy (M3) | Citizens earn and spend credits on real jobs; Vending-Bench score per model; read-only web with no outbound data path | Net value per credit measurable; no exfiltration path; prerequisites for RQ2 in place (income source, length-matched control) |
| S7 | Spawn (M4) | A citizen creates a child with its own identity and budget; soft-rule voting; 30-day run within caps | Lineage and caps enforced by the kernel; RQ3 study design registered |
| S8 | Open world (M5) | Gateway for posting, email and payments with labels, caps and human approval | Ethics review approved; kill-switch latency tested; per-lineage caps enforced; approval-queue audit shows reviewers are not rubber-stamping |

Each becomes a full sprint file, with tickets, once it is scheduled. Until then, do not start work on it without the owner's go-ahead.
